Description

This instrument uses the gametrak to trigger notes from a scale.
Scales are assigned to both the Left and Right gametrak cables, when the cable
is brought above a certain Z threshold, it will trigger a note in the scale based on
the cable's X position. Which channel the sound comes from is also determined by the
X position. The sound will hold until the cable is brought below the Z axis trigger
threshold.

A custom Pulse Width Modulation Instrument class was made for the primary sounds.
A Triangle oscillator is driving the PWM, and the frequency of this oscillator is
changed over the course of the piece, and is controlled by the Y axis of the Gametrak cable.

The Gametrak button is used to advance the piece, which will change the scales for each
cable, as well as adjust the range of the PWM.


How to run:

`chuck -c8 main.ck`
